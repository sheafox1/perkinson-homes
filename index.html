How It Works

How building with us works

From the first call to the keys.

1. Start the conversation

Tell us about your family, your lot and your dream home. We make the process as simple as possible.

2. Walk your lot

We make site visits before or after you buy the lot to help you decide on the best placement, garage orientation and more.

3. Design every detail

Our design team helps you choose each element of your home, from floor to ceiling.

4. Build with no surprises

Everything is tracked in CoConstruct, with up-front pricing and clear communication.

Our Story

Our story

Perkinson Homes is a family tradition: Brian Perkinson is a third-generation homebuilder who founded the company in 2008, during the housing downturn, and grew it through a love of unique custom homes. He and his wife Regina lead a team of supervisors, designers and support staff.
